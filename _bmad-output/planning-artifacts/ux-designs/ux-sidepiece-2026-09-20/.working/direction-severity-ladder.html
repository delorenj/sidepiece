Direction · The Unshipped Redesign

The sharpest thing 33GOD ever designed, finally shipped

On 2026-09-06 the Holocene IA redesign produced six artboards, IBM Plex, and a six-rung severity ladder where every hue is welded to a glyph — and a week later globals.css contained none of it. This direction takes those laws as binding and makes the Cockpit the surface where they finally land, which matters beyond one panel: if Sidepiece speaks the ladder, then Ticket state, Agent presence and Dispatched Command status become one vocabulary across the platform instead of the four incompatible ones Holocene audited itself for.

The law “density comes from leading, not from shrinking type” fights a 340px column, and I did not resolve it by shrinking. Leading stays generous (1.62–1.68 on every variable string, nothing under 11.5px); the budget comes back by deleting sections — the ladder legend lives on this sheet, not in the panel; ticket state is carried by a sticky group header and a grey mark instead of a word repeated forty-four times; the composer shows both classification values at once so nothing is held across a scroll. The panel is taller than a dense one would be. That is the correct trade when the counter-metric is open time: Jarad reads four lines and leaves.

The hero has exactly one hue in it. Forty-four backlog tickets and a live PM are all grey, because OK is grey; the single green mark is a dispatch that actually completed. A panel that lights up only when something is true is the opposite of a dashboard.

Why this state. It is the one that tests the ladder hardest: a single ◆ governs three panes with one message, every control beside it stays grey chrome, and the completed dispatch keeps its green because completing is a fact that an outage does not undo. Staleness is drawn as a dotted rule and the word “cached” — confidence is form, never hue, and no opacity is used anywhere, so the contrast figures in the token table are the real ones.

The six-rung ladder, as Sidepiece would ship it

One vocabulary for Ticket state, Agent presence and Dispatched Command status. Recovered verbatim from holocene/…/ia-redesign-2026-09/design/Ladder.dc.html, 2026-09-06.

◆blocked#ff5f6dBridge unreachable. A dispatch rejected. Nothing behind it can be true.
●broken#ff9152A named dependency failed — Registry, Plane, Bloodbank or the Agent gateway.
▲stuck#ffd166Time-derived only. A dispatch past its window; a Turn with no first token.
▪nudge#7fb0ffWants a human. Declared but unknown; a PM question waiting on the ticket.
─ok#6b7793Grey. Backlog is grey. A reachable PM is grey. Most of the panel is grey.
─cleared#69d3a5Left the queue in the last 6 hours. Green means exactly one thing.
  • A rung hue never appears on chrome, controls, filters, captions or explainers — only on a row, cell or badge whose severity it states.
  • No rung hue appears without its glyph.
  • OK is grey. Green means exactly one thing.
  • Confidence is form, never hue. No opacity — compute the blend.
  • 10.5px for static chrome at ≥4.5:1; 11.5px minimum for anything variable.
  • Two self-inflicted breaks, found while building this and left visible rather than patched: OK measures 4.23:1 against its own 4.5:1 floor, and OK and CLEARED share the glyph ─. Both are noted with proposed fixes in the token table at the top of this file.
