Sidepiece — four directions for the Cockpit

Each is a complete visual personality applied to the same screen: the Cockpit resolved on the Sidepiece project itself, at the real 340px panel width, with a genuine failure state beside it. Scroll each column. Nothing here is a palette swap — they differ on density, type, structure and register. Pick one, or name the parts you want from several.

01 · inherit the fleet
33GOD Inherited
Quiet deep-navy platform chrome. 1px borders, zero shadows, almost no colour until something has a state to report — and then the colour always arrives with its glyph.

forCloses a decision that has blocked two BMAD runs, and hands Sidepiece and Holocene the same DESIGN.md. Fixes Holocene's two named defects instead of copying them: one navy ramp, and a type system that actually loads.

againstInheritance from a parent whose own audit calls it broken. If Holocene never reconciles upstream, this isn't a sibling — it's a fifth palette that resembles the first.

02 · disappear
Chrome Native
Browser furniture. VS Code greys, Chrome's own UI font, structure from hairlines, no shadow, no radius past 2px, not one pixel of brand — the panel you stop noticing.

forThe only direction with a literal claim on SM-C1 — if the panel is built from Chrome's own parts there's nothing to re-orient to, so dipping in costs zero structurally rather than by argument. Free: the palette already exists in kapture, and no webfont to load or fail.

againstYou run DevTools constantly and will have both open. Two grey panels from the same palette are two panels you have to tell apart — reintroducing the exact orientation beat it claims to delete. And a product with no face is harder to stay excited about.

03 · ship the unshipped
The Severity Ladder
Severe, quiet, forensic. A dark navy instrument built entirely from 1px rules and grey text, where colour is rationed so hard the whole healthy screen contains exactly one hue.

forThe only direction that arrives with laws already written in your own voice — "OK is grey. Green means exactly one thing." "Confidence is form, never hue." Its reach is the real argument: adopt the six-rung ladder here and it becomes the platform's shared state vocabulary, which Holocene then inherits back.

againstIt's a revival, and it died once for a reason nobody recorded. Four of the six rungs never appear on a healthy screen — that may be more vocabulary than a solo operator's side panel can earn. Tickets land below the fold.

04 · argue with all of it
Deliberately Foreign
A printed instrument. A warm paper sheet laid on top of the page, set in serif, ruled in ink, signed with a fluorescent spine and crop marks that follow it out onto whatever site you're annotating.

forThe only one designing a register rather than a panel. Sidepiece is the one tool you own that draws on other people's screens, and an identity that stops at the panel edge doesn't serve the north star. The same spine and crop marks ride the Cockpit, the hover tooltip and the in-page bubble — a Sidepiece mark is yours on a white docs site and a black dashboard alike.

againstThe spot hue is the whole argument and the whole risk, and it can't be de-risked by recolouring — a quieter spot stops being unmistakable on both grounds. Costs 12% of the panel permanently for a signature. Inherits nothing, so it answers the inheritance question with "neither."
