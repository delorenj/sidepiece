### Finding: Bloodbank subject grammar invalidates both source documents

`BRAINDUMP.md` (`bloodbank.repo.<repo-name>`) and `docs/product-brief.md`
(`bloodbank.v1.repo.<repo-name>`) both describe a repo-scoped subject
subscription. Neither is legal: subjects are exactly 5 tokens with no version
and no identity tokens; repo identity lives in `data.repo`. Verified against
`_bmad/custom/workflows/ticket-lifecycle/data/event-schemas.md` and the
scrum-master retirement note, both post-dating the v1 purge (commits 815cb89
through a62acd2).

Consequence: a Project-scoped event view is a payload filter through Candystore,
not a subscription. Reshapes the deferred event feed and FR-7's outcome
correlation. Recorded in PRD §6 and addendum §B.1.

### Finding: `.project.json` rename may have broken a downstream reader

fixed, since it is not this PRD's call to make.
